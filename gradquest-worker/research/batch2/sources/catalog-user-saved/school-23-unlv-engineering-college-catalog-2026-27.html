Howard R. Hughes College of Engineering

At the Howard R. Hughes College of Engineering, our mission is: Educate, Engage, Inspire, and Innovate. We are dedicated to cultivating the next generation of technological leaders and prioneering solutions to global challenges.

As a graduate student, you will join a thriving and supportive community of scholars committed to cutting-edge research. We offer an environment designed for high achievement. 

  • World-Class Facilities: Conduct your research in our state-of-the-art building, which offers first-class research facilities.
  • Renowned Faculty: Work alongside world-renowned faculty who have earned national and international recognition in their fields.
  • High-Impact Research: Your research will directly lead to opportunities to present at major technical conferences, publish in leading journals, and collaborate with colleagues globally.
  • Open and Innovative Culture: We foster an open, welcoming environment dedicated to hands-on experimental learning and the pursuit of high-impact research and technological entrepreneurship.

Our graduate programs span the depth and breadth of modern Engineering and Computer Science.

Discipline Key Research Areas
Computer Science & Emerging Technology

Computer Science, Big Data, Artifical Intelligence, Machine Learning
and Deep Learning, and Cybersecurity.
Civil & Environmental  Construction Management, Environmental, Civil, Transportation,
Water Resources, and Air and Water Quality.
Core Engineering Disciplines Aerospace, Biomedical, Electrical, Materials, Mechanical,
and Nuclear Engineering.


Our faculty and students actively explore critical areas that are shaping the future of technology and society, including:

  • Autonomous Systems: Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) and Robotics
  • Advanced Materials: Nanomaterials and Devices
  • Digital Security: Big Data, Cybersecurity, Sensors and Systems, and National Security Applications
  • Sustainability: Renewable Energy, Air and Water Quality, and Water Resources

We believe in the power of collaboration. We are committed to engaging the community and collaborating with partners to reach creative solutions to problems facing the region, the country, and the world. 

These opportunities consistently launch our graduates into successful careers, leading them to work locally, nationally, and internationally in some of the top laboratories, research centers, universities, and corporations.

Rama Venkat, Ph.D., Dean
Mohamed Trabia, Ph.D., Associate Dean for Research and Graduate Studies
