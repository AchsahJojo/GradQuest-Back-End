H2: What makes me unique

TITLE: Star Icon

My expertise lies at the convergence of artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, digital forensics, and quantum-safe communication. My research focuses on building trustworthy and resilient AI systems to address real-world security challenges, with an emphasis on deepfake detection, secure multimedia analytics, and next-generation cyber defense.

I bring a unique combination of academic research, industry experience, and applied security practice. Prior to joining CSUEB, I worked as a Security Risk Analyst at VMware and contributed to federally funded research at the FINDS Digital Forensic Center of Excellence at Florida International University. This background allows me to translate theoretical advances into deployable, high-impact security solutions.

My work has been published in leading venues, including ACM DTRAP, IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics, IEEE MultiMedia, The Conversation , Elsevier, and Springer Nature. I am also a co-author of the book " Artificial Intelligence in Practice: Theory and Applications for Cyber Security and Forensics" , reflecting my commitment to advancing both foundational research and real-world applications of AI in security.

Beyond research, I am committed to inclusive education, student mentorship, and workforce development, with a focus on preparing students to design ethical, secure, and socially responsible AI technologies. I hold a Ph.D. and M.S. in Computer Science from Florida International University and actively collaborate with global academic, industry, and government partners to advance the future of secure and responsible computing.

H2: Professional Focus 
Artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, digital forensics, quantum-safe communication, secure multimedia systems, and resilient AI architectures.

Research interests include deepfake detection, cyber risk analytics, privacy-preserving computation, and the design of trustworthy, secure, and scalable AI-driven systems.
