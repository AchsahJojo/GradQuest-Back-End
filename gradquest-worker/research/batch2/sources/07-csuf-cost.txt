Online MSE students can get the same benefit/services with on-campus students, such as Health Center, Pollak Library, Student Recreational Center, Free Student Software, Student Technology Services, and etc.
To use campus services, get a student card (bring your picture ID to the Titan Card desk in the Pollak Library). For more details, please visit the getting a Titancard page . 

Note: The MSE costs listed are estimates that are subject to change upon different rates applied by the university. You may need to add other cost for study (application fee ($70), books (roughly $1,000 total), cost of living, etc.).
