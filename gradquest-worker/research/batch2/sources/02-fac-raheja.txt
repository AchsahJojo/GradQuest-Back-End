H2: About Me

I am a Professor and current Chair of the Department of Computer Science in the College of Science. 

My research interests are in the area of image processing, computer vision and machine learning. My research focuses on interdisciplinary applications of AI techniques.

I am a member of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society, Computer Society and the ACM .
