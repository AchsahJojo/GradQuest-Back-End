H2: About Me

Dr. Yunsheng Wang is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at California State Polytechnic University, Pomona, where he has been a faculty member since August 2022. Before joining Cal Poly Pomona, he was an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Kettering University.

He received his Ph.D. in Computer and Information Sciences from Temple University in 2013, his M.Res. in Telecommunications from University College London in 2008, and his B.Eng. in Electronic and Information Engineering from Dalian University of Technology in 2007.

His research interests include Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT), connected and autonomous driving, edge computing, cybersecurity, and social networks. His work spans wireless networks, mobile computing, cooperative AI inference, intelligent transportation systems, and vehicle edge computing.

Dr. Wang is Editor of the International Journal of Ad Hoc and Ubiquitous Computing. He was Secretary of the IEEE Computer Society Special Technical Community on Autonomous Driving Technologies. He is also General Co-Chair of the Fifth IEEE International Conference on Mobility: Operations, Services, and Technologies ( MOST 2027 ), to be held at CPP in April 2027.

His research has been supported by the National Science Foundation, the U.S. Department of Transportation, the U.S. Department of Justice, SAE International, General Motors, and Cal Poly Pomona programs. He leads the AIoT Lab at https://aiotlabcpp.github.io/AIoT-Lab/ .
