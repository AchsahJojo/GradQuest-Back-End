Dr. Shabnam Sodagari
Dr. Shabnam Sodagari is a Professor in the Computer Engineering and Computer Science Department at CSULB. She received her Ph.D. from the Pennsylvania State University. She is a senior member of IEEE and has several publications at impactful journals and major conferences. She has organized workshops and conferences on information security. She is on the Editorial Board of IEEE Vehicular Technology Society Open Journal. She has taught courses on information security, quantum computing, and machine learning.
