MINDs Lab Search this site Embedded Files Skip to main content Skip to navigation MINDs Lab Multidisciplinary Intelligence for Novel Data Solutions MINDs Lab develops artificial intelligence and data science methods for antimicrobial peptide discovery and qualitative data analysis. Our students explore complementary data representations, reliable learning methods, and interactive tools that help researchers make sense of complex data. About Me I’m an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at California State University, Los Angeles . My research explores AI for computational biology, health informatics, and human-computer interaction, with a focus on augmenting human capabilities and decision-making.
