Prior to SDSU, I was Assistant Professor of Computer Science in the School of Computing and Information Science (SCIS) at the University of Maine. She received the 2022 NSF CAREER Award, 2023 Maine College of Engineering and Computing (MCEC) Early Career Research Award, and the nomination of 2022 and 2024 Supervisor of the Year. Prior to UMaine, she was a postdoctoral research fellow in the Electrical Engineering and Computer Science Department at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. She has also held appointments in Brazil and Italy.
