ABOUT ME

I am a Professor of Computer Science at California State University, Los Angeles (CSULA). My research focuses on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science for various applications including sustainability, environment (wildfire, air quality), healthcare, traffic management, and other social good applications. Before joining CSULA, I was a Technical Project Manager and Senior Data Scientist at WANDA Inc. working on AI and Machine Learning  for Risk Prediction, Predictive Analytics modeling. Prior to this position, I was a Post-Doctoral Reseacher and Research Manager at UCLA Computer Science Department working on AI, Predictive Analytics, Advanced Machine Learning, and Wireless Health Technologies. Before joining UCLA, I was a Research Scientist with Cornell University doing research on AI, Big Data, Machine Learning, Statistical Signal Processing, and Wireless Sensor Networks.

I am the Founding Director of  Artificial Intelligence & Data Science Research Lab  at California State University Los Angeles. For more information about our research and projects, visit our lab website: Artificial Intelligence & Data Science Research Lab.
