About

Welcome!

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science in the College of Engineering, Computer Science, and Technology at California State University, Los Angeles. In addition to teaching, I conduct research in the areas of human computer interaction (HCI) and design, accessibility, and assistive technology. I serve as the Director of Industry Partnerships for the CERES Network.

I am an alumnus of UC Irvine, Carnegie Mellon University, and DePaul University. I also help run The Gus and Buddy Foundation and paddle the waters of Southern California with the fine folks at The Makapo Aquatics Project
