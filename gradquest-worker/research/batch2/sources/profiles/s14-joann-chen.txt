Bio Dr. Chen’s research interests center around Differential Privacy (DP), privacy-enhancing technologies, and privacy in machine learning. She has experience in working on differentially private DNS resolution, DP for data stream release, differentially private resource allocator, and quantifying privacy risks in machine learning.
