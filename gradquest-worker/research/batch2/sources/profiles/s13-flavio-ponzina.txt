Dr. Flavio Ponzina | Electrical and Computer Engineering | SDSU Dr. Flavio Ponzina Assistant Professor Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering College of Engineering Academic Affairs SDSU More on this Profile Contact About Publications and Presentations Email Primary Email: [email protected] Phone/Fax Primary Phone: 619-594-0073 Building/Location Engineering - 410 5500 Campanile Drive San Diego, CA 92182 Website Links Website Back to Top Bio Flavio Ponzina is an Assistant Professor in the Electrical and Computer Engineering department at San Diego State University. He obtained his PhD degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne in 2023, and he then joined the SEELab at UC San Diego as a postdoctoral scholar. His research interests include HW-SW co-design optimizations for edge AI, unsupervised, ensemble, and distributed learning, energy-efficient systems, and processing in-memory acceleration on emerging memory technologies. Education Ph.D., École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), Switzerland, 2023.
