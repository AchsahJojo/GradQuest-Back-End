Huiping Guo | Cal State LA Skip to main content California State University, Los Angeles Huiping Guo College of Engeering, Computer Science, and Technology Department of Computer Science Office ET 318 Phone (323) 343-6673 Email [email protected] Dr. Huiping Guo received her B.S. and M.S. in Computer Science from Nanjing University of Aeronautics & Astronautics in 1992 and 1999, respectively. From 1999 to 2003, she was a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at DISCOVER Lab , University of Ottawa , under Dr. Nicolas D. Georganas. From 2003 to 2005, she worked as a postdoctoral fellow at the Center for Secure Information Systems , George Mason University , supervised by Dr. Sushil Jajodia. In September 2005, she joined the Computer Science Department , at California State University in Los Angeles as an Assitant Professor. She has been a full professor since 2016.
