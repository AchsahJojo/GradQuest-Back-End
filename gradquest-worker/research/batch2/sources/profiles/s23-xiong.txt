He received his Ph.D. degree from the Department of Computer Science at Georgia State University in 2023 under the supervision of Dr. Wei Li and Dr. Zhipeng Cai. He received M.E. degree and B.S. degree from the Department of Computer Science, Harbin Engineering University in 2019 and Department of Mathematics, Northeast Forestry University in 2016, respectively. His research interests lie in a broad area of cybersecurity and privacy, including privacy-preserving machine learning, privacy-preserving data mining, the Internet of Things (IoT), and data security related topics. He has published papers on and served as reviewer for bunch of premier conferences and journals, including IEEE ICDM , AAAI , IEEE GLOBECOM , IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology (TVT) , IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics (TII) , Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE) , Transactions on Network Science and Engineering (TNSE) , IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC) , etc. Research Areas Data Privacy, Cybersecurity, Machine Learning, the Internet of Things Related Links Personal Web Google Scholar
