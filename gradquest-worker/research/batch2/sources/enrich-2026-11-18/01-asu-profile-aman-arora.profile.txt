[bio]
Aman Arora is an assistant professor in the School of Computing and Augmented Intelligence in the Ira A. Fulton Schools of Engineering. His research interests are in the areas of reconfigurable computing (primarily FPGAs), machine learning (ML) and artificial intelligence (AI) acceleration, and using machine learning (ML) and artificial intelligence (AI) for chip design/EDA. He has more than 10 years of experience in the semiconductor industry.
