[bio]
Adil Ahmad is an assistant professor in the School of Computing and Augmented Intelligence (SCAI) at ASU. Before joining ASU in the fall of 2022, Ahmad spent many enjoyable years obtaining his doctorate from Purdue University, where he was fortunate to work with Pedro Fonseca and Byoungyoung Lee. During his doctoral studies, Ahmad also greatly enjoyed interning at Microsoft Research and NEC Laboratories America, Inc., working alongside Sangho Lee, Marcus Peinado, and Chung Hwan Kim.
