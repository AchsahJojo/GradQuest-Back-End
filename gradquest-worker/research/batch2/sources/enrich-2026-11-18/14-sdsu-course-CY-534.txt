The class will be offered for letter grade with no option to take it for Cr/NC Prerequisite(s): CS 210, CY 302, and MATH 254; or graduate standing; or instructor consent Fundamentals of cryptography and its applications on the blockchain technology and cryptocurrency. Topics include hash functions, public key cryptography, digital signatures, blockchain ledger, consensus protocols, smart contracts, decentralized applications, decentralized finance, and state-of-the-art security problems.
