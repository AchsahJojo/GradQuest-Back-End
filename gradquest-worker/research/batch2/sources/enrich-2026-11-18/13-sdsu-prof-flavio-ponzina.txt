He obtained his PhD degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne in 2023, and he then joined the SEELab at UC San Diego as a postdoctoral scholar. His research interests include HW-SW co-design optimizations for edge AI, unsupervised, ensemble, and distributed learning, energy-efficient systems, and processing in-memory acceleration on emerging memory technologies. Education Ph.D., École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), Switzerland, 2023.
