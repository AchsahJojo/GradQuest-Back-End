Topics include full lexical, syntactic, and semantic analysis; neural models such as RNNs, LSTMs, GRUs, and transformers; contextual embeddings (e.g., BERT, GPT); retrieval-augmented and multilingual NLP applications. Emphasis on theoretical foundations, implementation of advanced models, and engagement with current research literature. Prerequisite(s): CS major and C or better in CS 3310 ; or consent of instructor.
