I develop methods for trustworthy distributed learning, focusing on verifiable evaluation, post-quantum authentication, and resilience to malicious participants. My work spans federated learning, IoT, and edge systems.

Prospective students: CSULB undergraduate and MS students interested in this work can apply through the TIDES Lab interest form.

Research

Verifiable distributed learning

Zero-knowledge proofs that verify whether a local evaluation metric meets a threshold without revealing the metric.

Post-quantum security

Cryptographic foundations that prepare federated, decentralized, and edge systems for quantum threats.

Adversarial resilience

Defenses and incentives for systems in which participants may poison, manipulate, or game the protocol.
