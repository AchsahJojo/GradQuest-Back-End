Earning Your Cal State East Bay Degree

Welcome to our university! We are proud to provide this catalog, a comprehensive overview of the vibrant and relevant degree and certificate programs currently granted by Cal State East Bay. You will see we offer more than 80 degrees at the bachelor’s and master’s level as well as 31 credential and certificate programs. We are proud to include a doctoral program in education with an emphasis in educational leadership.
