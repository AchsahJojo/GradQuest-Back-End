new window) CECS 698 - Thesis or Industrial Project (3-6 units) Prerequisite: Advancement to Candidacy. Computer Engineering MS, Computer Science MS, and Engineering MS students only. Planning, preparation, completion of thesis or equivalent industrial project report on a suitable topic in computer engineering and computer science following the library's prescribed format. Requires consultation with Graduate Advisor and submission of Agreement for Independent Study form each semester. Both grading options. May be repeated to a maximum of 6 units in the same semester.
