[bio]
Pooyan Fazli is an assistant professor and the director of the People and Robots Laboratory (PeRL) in The GAME School and the Media and Immersive eXperience (MIX) Center at Arizona State University. He received his Ph.D. in computer science from the University of British Columbia. Previously, he was a postdoctoral fellow at Carnegie Mellon University and the University of British Columbia. His research focuses on artificial intelligence, robot learning, vision and language, multimodal learning, and video understanding.
