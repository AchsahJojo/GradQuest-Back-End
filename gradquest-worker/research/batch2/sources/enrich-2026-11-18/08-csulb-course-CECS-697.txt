- Directed Research (1-3 units) Prerequisite: Open to Computer Engineering MS, Computer Science MS, or Engineering MS students only. Theoretical and experimental problems in computer science and engineering requiring extensive research. Advancement to candidacy and program GPA of at least 3.0 are required. Graduate advisor and project supervisor must be consulted prior to registration. Independent Study. Credit/No Credit grading only.
