Engineering success

Our faculty team includes technological trailblazers, inspirational innovators and some of the world’s top computer scientists. Working across diverse specialties — from artificial intelligence to cybersecurity to robotics —and working in many domains, they are internationally recognized for their research, conducting many funded, state-of-the-art research projects for both government and industry. They are not only involved in leading-edge research from both methodological and applications perspectives, but they also actively advise students, teach innovation and continuously improve their curriculum.
