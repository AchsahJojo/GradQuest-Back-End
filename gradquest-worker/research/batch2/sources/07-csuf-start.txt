H2: Before you apply, please check that you meet the following requirements:

- 

H2: University Requirements:

- The applicant must meet the minimum criteria for the admission.

- The applicant must have completed four-year college course of study and hold an acceptable baccalaureate degree with a minimum/cumulative grade point average of 2.5 for applicants having an undergraduate degree in Computer Science or 3.0 for other degree holders.

- An official transcript is required from each university or college you have attended.

- For international students: a minimum score of 550 (paper) or 80 in TOEFL or 6.5 Academic in IELTS or 58 in PTE.

- For further details, please visit graduate admissions. 

- 

H2: Program Requirements:

- The applicant should have taken an Introductory Software Engineering course (equivalent with CPSC 362 
at CSUF) or should have work experience in software development.

- If you did not complete the above Software Engineering course, email your resume to the MSE Program Coordinator at [email protected] 
to check your qualifications.

- If you would like to know more about the program, please email [email protected] 
.

H2: If you meet both the above requirements, apply using the following steps:

- Apply online at the apply page 
. The online application will be open from October 1, 2025 to May 1, 2026 . A $70 application fee will apply and must be paid online.

- Submit all your supporting documents (official transcripts from all institutions attended, mark sheets, degree certificate, and official TOEFL/ IETLS /PTE scores) to the following address:

California State University, Fullerton 
Office of Admissions 
800 N State College Blvd 
Langsdorf Hall Room 111 
Fullerton, CA 92831 

Documents may be sent electronically to [email protected] . Documents must be sent directly from the school/institution of origin. 
For more details, please visit graduate admission 
.
Note: Usually it takes a few weeks or months to get the result. First come, first served. Therefore, apply as early as possible.

H2: After you get an admission letter:

- The deadline to accept the offer of admission and pay a non-refundable $250 enrollment deposit is TBD.
Note: After completing this step, you might have to wait for a few days for the process to be completed.

- Enroll in the classes (usually starting from April - to be notified). For more information on the enrollment process, please visit the enroll page 
.

- Attend the MSE Orientation on campus* (usually on the 1st Saturday starting the Fall semester - to be notified). 
We strongly recommend you attend the Orientation as it is an excellent opportunity to form groups with your classmates for your group projects and meet the professors in person.

- Attend the class on time (check the university calendar and your university email account)

*In case you cannot attend the Orientation for some reasons, you can review the Orientation video later
