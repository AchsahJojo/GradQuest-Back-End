H1: First Semester Masters Student Planner

This tool is NOT a substitute for attending orientation or booking an appointment with the Computer Science graduate coordinator. It gives a suggested starting point for your first semester based on the prerequisite/remediation courses listed in your admission letter.

H2: Step 1 — Select your outstanding courses

Check every prerequisite or remediation course listed in your conditional admission letter . Leave a course unchecked if you have already completed it with a B‑ or better.
