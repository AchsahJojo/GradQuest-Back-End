Graduate Programs

Master of Science in Computer Engineering

The Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering offers a graduate program leading to a Master of Science degree in Computer Engineering (MSCompE). 

Master of Science in Electrical Engineering

The Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering offers graduate study leading to a Master of Science degree in Electrical Engineering (MSEE).

Joint Doctoral Program

The Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) faculty at SDSU are actively involved in research projects with graduate students. We offer a Joint Doctoral Program (JDP) in collaboration with the University of California, San Diego (UCSD).
