Recommended 2-Year Roadmaps
Graduation roadmaps are suggested academic plans. Students should design their own plans to complete the degree requirements in a timely fashion.
Check the scheduling patterns of the courses and ensue that the CS 4xxx prerequisite course is completed prior to the CS 5xxx course.
It is strongly recommended to complete no more than three courses during any term.
Courses in the summer term can be taken to accelerate completion of the requirements.
Academic Status and Progression
Conditionally Classified Graduate Standing (G1)
: Students given prerequisite courses as part of their provisional admission must complete these courses in their first semester following the recommendation of their advisor. After completing the prerequisites, students are required to submit a completed
GS-8 form
to their graduate faculty advisor to transition their classification status from
Conditionally Classified (G1) to Classified (G2)
.
Classified Graduate Standing (G2):
Review the program requirements and the suggested roadmap. Ensure you complete the degree requirements while maintaining a minimum term GPA of 3.0.
Advance to Candidacy (G3):
Students ready to enroll in a thesis or take the comprehensive exam should advance to candidacy (from
G2 to G3
). The minimum requirement for candidacy advancement are the completion of 12 units and a minimum GPA of 3.0. To advance, students must submit a completed
GS-10 form
. It is recommended that students submit this form during the registration period for the term in which they plan to start their thesis or take the exam.
Thesis course (CS 5990) registration
CS 5990 should be taken
exactly twice
.
First time registration: Upon advancement to candidacy, submit a
CS 5990 request form
during the registration period for the first term in which you plan to start the thesis.
Second time registration: Submit a
CS 5990 request form
during the registration period for the second term in which you plan to complete the thesis. There is no need to advance to candidacy a second time.
Optional step: Students who have been advanced to candidacy and have completed all coursework including 6-units of thesis but need additional time to complete their thesis, must be registered in
UNIV 9000
.
