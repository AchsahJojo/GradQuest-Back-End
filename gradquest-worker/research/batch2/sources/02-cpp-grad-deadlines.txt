H2: Application Filing Period

Spring 2027 applications open July 1, 2026 

Fall 2027 applications open on August 1, 2026 

International applicants: For graduate programs that have a Fall application deadline beyond 5/1, international applicants must submit an application no later than 5/1 to ensure sufficient time to obtain a visa. 

Apply online via Cal State Apply 

For more information about your Graduate program and program Admission requirements, please directly contact your Graduate Coordinator.
