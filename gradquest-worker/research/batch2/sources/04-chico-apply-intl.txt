H1: How to Apply: International Graduate

Take the next step in your educational journey by joining the Wildcat Family. Chico State’s international student master’s application is open for fall (August) and spring (January) start through Cal State Apply, the application for all CSU campuses. 
Start Application 

H2: Application Open Dates*
Fall (August) Start: August 1–April 1 
Spring (January) Start: July 1–October 1
*Master's program deadlines vary. Some programs offer fall admission only. Please check the application deadline for your specific graduate program .

H2: Get Started

- Review Admissions Criteria 
Admissions requirements for graduate international students include completion of an equivalent to a US 4-year bachelor’s degree and English proficiency (if applicable). Additional exams may be required depending on the program, such as GRE.

- Submit Your Application
Start application online via Cal State Apply. Visit the Help Center for application tips, troubleshooting, and more. When creating your account, please select "F-1 visa" as an international student.

- Pay Your Fee
There is a $70 application fee.

- Submit Supporting Documents
Submit all required documents within two weeks of the application deadline. Only complete applications will be reviewed. All materials submitted during the application process become University property.

- Send official academic records by international mail/courier: Official degree certificates and transcripts from all universities attended after high/secondary school should be mailed to Chico State. 

- Applicants with an Indian bachelor's degree must submit university-attested academic records (not college-attested).

- Applicants with a Chinese bachelor's degree must also submit a graduation certificate.

- Upload Digital Supporting Documents 

- Professional word-for-word English translation of all academic records (if university issued in a language other than English)

- Financial support documents (not required for admission)

- Transmit official exam scores: 
- Proof of English language proficiency 

- You only need to submit GRE scores if you are applying for the Computer Science MS and your bachelor’s degree is not from an ABET accredited school in a computer science related discipline. (Choose university code “4048” to digitally send official scores)

- Complete additional program applications (if applicable): 
Some programs require additional online applications or document submission directly to the faculty department. Review the graduate program page for details.

- Keep in Touch 
Make sure you add apply@csuchico.edu to your contact list. Email is our primary form of communication and how we will keep in contact with you throughout the admissions process.

H2: Application Processing
We begin review of graduate applications once all supporting documents have been received. Some programs have a single review period for the cycle and others have a rolling admission process.

- Check Your Status 
After applying online, log in to your Portal and navigate to the Student Center to check your admission status. An Admissions Evaluator will also communicate with you by email throughout the admissions process.

- Submit Additional Documents (if requested)
If your application is complete, we may have enough information to make an admission decision. If, after reviewing your application, it is determined we need more information, including other transcripts or test scores, we will notify you by email with instructions and a specific deadline.

- Accept Admissions Offer 
Once you have been offered admission, commit to Chico State by submitting your intent to enroll.

H3: 

What are “official” transcripts?

US document standards are different than other countries and education systems.
To be considered official, a transcript must be in a sealed envelope and be:

- Issued directly from the secondary/high school or university/college;

- A certified copy issued by a government office (example: Kuwait Cultural Office); or

- Sent electronically via an official transcript transmission agency like Parchment
See International Admissions Documents and Standards for further details.

H3: 

Do I need to submit Financial Support Documents at the time of application?

No, financial documents are not initially required. We request these documents so that if you are admitted, we can quickly issue your Form i-20 and you can move forward with the F-1 visa application process.

H3: 

What if I don’t meet the English Proficiency Requirements?

Applicants who don’t meet the English language requirements may apply for conditional admission by enrolling in a language program at one of our partner schools. When the appropriate language proficiency is reached, students can then begin their academic program at the University.
The advantages to conditional admission?

- No English language proficiency exam is required! That means, no TOEFL or IELTS score is needed for you to attend. We will accept successful completion of a partner language school program as a substitute for a TOEFL or IELTS score.

- You can begin your studies in the US now! Conditional admission allows you to begin your US classroom experience right away. Studies show language proficiency increases much more rapidly with full language and cultural immersion. 
Learn more about language proficiency requirements .

H3: 
Honoring the Mechoopda People

We acknowledge and are mindful that Chico State stands on lands that were originally occupied by the first people of this area, the Mechoopda, and we recognize their distinctive spiritual relationship with this land, the flora, the fauna, and the waters that run through campus. We are humbled that our campus resides upon sacred lands that since time immemorial have sustained the Mechoopda people and continue to do so today.
